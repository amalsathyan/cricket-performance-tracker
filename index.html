WeekendOne match (third cricket session)

Move gym days around your match. Avoid demanding leg training in the 48 hours before a match when possible. The match counts as one of your three cricket sessions.

Training priorities
Maintain strength with two efficient gym sessions; let the two practices and weekend match provide cricket-specific skill and conditioning. Do not add extra bowling just to hit a target.

Eight-week focus

  1. Weeks 1–2: establish comfortable loads and log cricket workload.
  2. Weeks 3–4: small strength progressions if recovery is good.
  3. Weeks 5–6: maintain strength; reduce gym volume during heavy match periods.
  4. Weeks 7–8: consolidate and review training, symptoms and performance.
